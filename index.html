Questions

Before you book.

What exactly is a Skill Lab?

A 90-minute evening introduction to a specific vocational skill. It combines a working practitioner's perspective with a practical challenge and an explanation of the longer career pathway.

Do I need previous experience?

No previous trade experience is assumed for the taster experience. The purpose is to explore the work before deciding whether to pursue further training.

Will I become qualified after 90 minutes?

No. The Skill Lab is a discovery experience, not a qualification. Formal pathways for these occupations require substantially longer training and, in some cases, certification.

How much does it cost?

The initial launch benchmark is €25–€60 per 90-minute session. Final pricing should be validated with actual customer demand.

Which skills can I try?

The initial programme focuses on Retrofit Installation, Heat Pump & HVAC, and Electrical.
