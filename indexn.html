THE GAP

Career information tells you what a job is. Experience tells you if it's yours.

Skill Lab sits between “I'm curious” and “I'm ready to commit” — a short, practical test of the work itself.

01

See the reality

Meet a working tradesperson and get beyond job titles, brochures and generic career descriptions.

02

Do something real

Handle tools, test ideas and solve a small practical challenge rather than sitting through another information session.

03

Decide with evidence

Leave knowing more about the work and whether you want to investigate the pathway further.

THE EXPERIENCE

Less presentation. More doing.

A simple rhythm designed to turn curiosity into useful evidence.

00–15

Meet the problem

Understand the real-world challenge and what the trade is trying to solve.

15–70

Get your hands on it

Use the tools, work through a practical challenge and experience the thinking involved.

70–90

Ask & decide

Talk pathways, training and day-to-day reality — then decide what you investigate next.
